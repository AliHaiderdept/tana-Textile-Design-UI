Philosophy & Lineage

Human Hands Meet Mathematical Precision

Every weave in our system begins as an actual physical sample in our studio, sourced from natural fibers like flax, mulberry silk, and hand-spun merino. We analyze the weave patterns under magnifying counters to trace exact coordinate pathways.

We then translate those real physical physics into raw SVG coordinates. When you use SŪTRA, you are interacting with structural vector physics that trace directly back to physical weft density and real tension curves.
